❓ How to play

Move: tap anywhere on the map to walk there. Drag to look around, pinch (or scroll) to zoom. A dashed line + 🚩 flag shows your route, with the distance left ("🚶 1.2km to go") — the flag gently bobs as you walk, and a zone sheet's 🚶 Walk button shows an estimated walk time at game speed (~N sec/min). No swipe-to-move, no D-pad — tap-to-move only.

📜 Daily quest: one quest per game day (work a shift, visit a place, eat, have fun, study, save, or sleep) — finish it for a cash reward on the quest card, including the new Study once, Have fun anywhere, Sleep once to rest and Save ₦5,000 in the bank quests and the Visit National Museum, Visit Igun Street, Visit Ekiosa Market, Visit Central Hospital, Visit Sapele Road, Visit Ugbowo, Visit Kada Plaza, Visit Ogbemudia Stadium, Visit Benin Airport, Visit New Benin Market and Visit Ring Road quests — now covering all 17 zones. Today's visit destination pulses gold on the map and carries a 📜 tag in 🔭 Places. City event: one rotating event per day (Market Day, Owambe, Rainy Day, NEPA Outage) with a small bonus shown on the banner.

⭐ XP & levels: work +20 XP, fun +15, study +40; a level every 150 XP. Achievements (First Shift, Certified, Homeowner, Business Owner, Governor) appear in your profile once earned — never claimed before you earn them.

🔭 Street finds: cash, snacks, drinks, 📱 airtime (sell it for ₦400) and 🍊 fruit pop up around you — walk into the teal circle and tap to grab them.

Low stats: a bar under 25% pulses amber/red and warns you once — eat, rest or have fun before it bottoms out. Rent: due every game day (5 real minutes) — visit GRA Housing. Miss it 3 times and you're under the bridge.

Menu ☰: bank (savings + loans), businesses (daily income), garage (keke & cars = faster), elections (run for Governor!), dice betting, street runs (risky 😅). Social: tap any player for their profile, ✉️ to DM them privately. ⚙️ Settings: sound on/off, reduce motion, and the home-screen install prompt.
